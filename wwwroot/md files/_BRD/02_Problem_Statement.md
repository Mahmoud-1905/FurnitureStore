# 2. Problem Statement

## 2.1 The Retail Disconnect
Single-store, independent furniture retailers often rely entirely on walk-in traffic. Without a comprehensive digital catalog, they remain invisible to the modern consumer who begins their shopping journey online. This lack of digital presence results in significant lost sales opportunities.

## 2.2 The E-commerce Trust Barrier
Conversely, purely online furniture sales face massive hurdles. High-ticket items like sofas, dining tables, and beds suffer from an industry-average **87% cart abandonment rate**. 

This high abandonment is driven by a fundamental lack of trust and the inability to physically verify key product attributes:
- **Material Quality:** Customers cannot feel the texture of fabrics or the finish of wood.
- **Scale and Proportions:** Digital photos often fail to accurately convey how a piece will fit into a specific living space.
- **Comfort:** The physical comfort of seating or bedding cannot be assessed through a screen.

## 2.3 Operational Inefficiencies
Store managers currently lack a unified system to seamlessly track online inquiries, manage in-store inventory, and schedule customer visits. Relying on disjointed tools (like physical ledgers or basic spreadsheets) leads to double-booking, stock discrepancies, and a disjointed customer experience.

## 2.4 Conclusion
There is a critical need for a platform that resolves the trust barrier of online furniture shopping while modernizing the retailer's operational capabilities. The absence of such a system directly harms both consumer confidence and business revenue.
